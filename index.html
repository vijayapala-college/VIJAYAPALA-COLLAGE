Get to know us ↗

Around 1912 · Early beginnings

Twelve children, one veranda

Vijayapala Maha Vidyalaya began with 12 children in the veranda of the home of Mr. T. B. Ekanayake, a respected resident of Harasgama. In the context of the time, the school was established primarily to provide religious instruction, under the leadership of Thalgahagoda Pemas Appuhamy.

1930 · Government takeover

A school in public service

The school was taken over by the government, with Mr. K. B. Ekanayake serving as its first head teacher. Five teachers taught classes from Grade 1 to Grade 5.

Through the years · Development

Growing with its community

Initially housed in a modest building with wattle-and-daub walls and a coconut-palm-leaf roof, the school gradually developed with government assistance and the support of parents. Today, its Advanced Level provision includes Arts, Commerce, Science and Mathematics streams.

“Around 1912” is an approximate date, reflecting the historical information supplied by the school.
